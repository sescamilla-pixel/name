How to use CircuitVerse

  1. Use the simulator on this page, or open the full project from the link in the footer.
  2. Set the A switches (Left Pair Switch) to the first number in binary. A15 is on the left and A0 is on the right.
  3. Set the B switches (Right Pair Switch) to the second number in binary, in the same order.
  4. Set M to 0 for addition or 1 for subtraction.
  5. Read the result bits from R15 on the left to R0 on the right.
  6. Read Cout. In addition, 1 means carry. In subtraction, 1 means no borrow and 0 means borrow.

Adds and subtracts two 16-bit binary numbers, A and B, using 16 full adders chained together. Each adder passes its carry to the next one.

The M switch selects the operation. M = 0 adds A and B, while M = 1 subtracts B from A using XOR gates and the carry-in.

Reading Carry and Borrow Cout shows the final carry or borrow. In addition, 1 means carry. In subtraction, 1 means no borrow, while 0 means borrow. For example, 3 − 5 produces FFFE, which represents -2 in 16-bit form.
